Repository ownership and visibility do not establish blanket publication rights.

Other retail/YouCam projects are excluded. Their media or provider outputs are not
robot training data. TetrisDog-Developer and TetrisDog-Robotics README retrieval
failed during inspection; no integration or validation claim is made for them.

TWS-Belief has no model calls in this demonstration. Source reliability settings
are configured scores, not calibrated probabilities. Synthetic counterfactual
replay is not physical causal identification. No ARMBench real-data result is
claimed merely by importing the upstream package.

The full private working copy retains LICENSE_SCOPE.md for its existing source
boundaries. The filtered submission candidate excludes the uncleared private
modules and carries its own LICENSE, NOTICE and vendored MIT notice. The MIT grant
does not relicense other components. See EDGE_INTEGRATION.md for the boundary
between the existing MCP service and the proposed edge-runtime integration.
